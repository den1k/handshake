At work

Co-founders, clients and freelancers. Decisions in writing, signed when it matters.

  • FoundersHiring decisions need both founders.
  • EquityShares vest over four years with a one-year cliff. Legally binding
  • ClientsNeither partner takes on a competing client without the other’s agreement. Legally binding

How it works

  1. 1. Write a ruleIn plain words, in an agreement on one topic.
  2. 2. Check itHandshake points out rules it clashes with and cases you haven’t covered.
  3. 3. Everyone agreesIt comes into force once every member has agreed.
  4. 4. It staysNothing is edited or erased. A new rule replaces an old one, and the history is kept.

When it has to hold up, make an agreement legally binding: everyone signs with their typed name, each signature is tied to a fingerprint of the exact text, and every rule gets a printable certificate.
