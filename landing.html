How it works

  1. Write a rule

    In plain words, inside an agreement on one topic.

  2. Check it

    Handshake points out rules it clashes with and cases you haven’t covered.

  3. Everyone agrees

    It comes into force once every member has agreed.

  4. It stays

    Nothing is edited or erased. A new rule replaces an old one, and the history is kept.

When it has to hold up

Make an agreement legally binding: everyone signs with their typed name, each signature is tied to a fingerprint of the exact text, and every rule gets a printable certificate with the full audit trail.
